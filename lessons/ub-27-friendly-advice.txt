title: Giving Friendly Advice
episode: UB27
scene: park

[story]
Marinette: Je suis fatiguée. Je travaille trop en ce moment. | I'm tired. I work too much at the moment.
Adrien: Tu devrais prendre quelques jours de repos. | You should take a few days of rest.
Marinette: Mais j'ai beaucoup de travail. Que ferais-tu à ma place ? | But I have a lot of work. What would you do in my place?
Adrien: À ta place, j'en parlerais à mon chef. | In your place, I would talk to my boss about it.
Marinette: Tu as raison. Il vaudrait mieux parler maintenant que trop tard. | You're right. It would be better to speak now than too late.
Adrien: Voilà. Et tu pourrais aussi marcher un peu chaque soir. | There you go. And you could also walk a little each evening.

[grammar]
grammar: The present conditional | Take the futur stem and add the imparfait endings: -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient. Parler becomes je parlerais, tu parlerais, il parlerait, nous parlerions, vous parleriez, ils parleraient. Irregular stems are the same as in the futur: être becomes je serais, avoir becomes j'aurais, aller becomes j'irais, faire becomes je ferais, pouvoir becomes je pourrais, devoir becomes je devrais, vouloir becomes je voudrais.
example: Tu devrais te reposer. | You should rest.
example: Je ferais la même chose. | I would do the same.
grammar: Advice expressions | Soft ways to advise: tu devrais / vous devriez plus infinitive, tu pourrais / vous pourriez plus infinitive, à ta place, je plus conditional, il vaudrait mieux plus infinitive, pourquoi tu ne pas ? These are gentler than the imperative: repose-toi is an order, tu devrais te reposer is advice.
example: À ta place, je dormirais. | In your place, I would sleep.
example: Il vaudrait mieux attendre. | It would be better to wait.
