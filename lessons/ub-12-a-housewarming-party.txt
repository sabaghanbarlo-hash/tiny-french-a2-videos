title: A Housewarming Party

[story]
Adrien: Tu m'invites à ta pendaison de crémaillère ? | Are you inviting me to your housewarming?
Marinette: Bien sûr ! Je t'ai écrit un message hier. Je te donne l'adresse. | Of course! I wrote you a message yesterday. I'll give you the address.
Adrien: Qu'est-ce que je peux t'apporter ? | What can I bring you?
Marinette: Apporte-moi quelque chose à boire, ça suffit. | Bring me something to drink, that's enough.
Adrien: Et les voisins ? Tu leur as demandé de venir ? | And the neighbours? Did you ask them to come?
Marinette: Oui, je leur ai parlé hier. Ils arrivent à vingt heures. | Yes, I spoke to them yesterday. They arrive at eight p.m.

[grammar]
grammar: Indirect object pronouns | Verbs followed by à plus a person use indirect pronouns: me, te, lui, nous, vous, leur. They go before the verb: je t'ai écrit, je lui parle, je leur ai demandé. Lui and leur do not change for gender, and the past participle does not agree with them.
example: Je lui parle demain. | I'll speak to him / her tomorrow.
example: Je leur ai demandé de venir. | I asked them to come.
grammar: Commands with pronouns | In an affirmative command the pronoun comes after the verb with a hyphen, and me becomes moi: apporte-moi, donne-lui, écris-leur. In a negative command it stays before the verb: ne me donne pas, ne lui parle pas.
example: Apporte-moi quelque chose. | Bring me something.
example: Ne leur dis rien. | Don't tell them anything.
