GRAMMAR
The futur simple - Take the infinitive and add ai, as, a, ons, ez, ont: je partirai, tu partiras, il partira, nous partirons, vous partirez, ils partiront. For -re verbs drop the final e: prendre becomes je prendrai. Irregular stems: être becomes ser-, avoir becomes aur-, aller becomes ir-, faire becomes fer-, venir becomes viendr-, pouvoir becomes pourr-, voir becomes verr-. The endings are always the same.
Si + present, then futur - To say what will happen if a condition is met, use si plus present in the condition and the futur in the result: s'il fait beau, on fera une promenade. Si becomes s' only before il / ils: s'il pleut, mais si elle vient. Do not use the futur after si.
