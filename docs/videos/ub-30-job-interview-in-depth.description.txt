VOCABULARY

perfectionniste (pehr-fehk-syoh-NEEST) - perfectionist
déléguer (day-lay-GAY) - to delegate
permettre de (pehr-MEH-truh duh) - to allow, to enable

GRAMMAR
Qui and que - Qui replaces the subject of the relative clause: un poste qui correspond à mes compétences. Que replaces the direct object: le travail que je commence. Before a vowel, que becomes qu': le travail qu'elle commence. With a composed tense, the participle agrees with the antecedent of que: les lettres que j'ai écrites.
Answering interview questions - Be positive and specific. Say what you are looking for, what you are good at and what you are working on. Use vous with the interviewer and Merci pour cette opportunité to finish.
