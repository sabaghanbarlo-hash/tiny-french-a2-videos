VOCABULARY
changer de ville (shahn-ZHAY duh VEEL) - to move to a different city
l'année dernière (lah-NAY dehr-NYEHR) - last year
cette année (seht ah-NAY) - this year
la confiance (lah kohn-FYAHNSS) - confidence
fier / fière (FYEHR) - proud
réussir (ray-ew-SEER) - to succeed
bravo (brah-VOH) - well done
félicitations (fay-lee-see-tah-SYOHN) - congratulations
bonne continuation (bohn kohn-tee-new-ah-SYOHN) - all the best, keep it up

GRAMMAR
Choosing the right tense - Use the passé composé for events and changes, the imparfait for habits and background in the past, the présent for now, aller plus infinitive for the near future, the futur simple for the further future, and the conditionnel for wishes and unreal situations.
Congratulating and ending a conversation - Congratulate with bravo, félicitations and with pour: félicitations pour ton diplôme. Wish someone well with bonne continuation, bonne chance, à bientôt. Thank someone with merci pour tout, merci pour ton aide. You are now ready for A2 and for the next level of French.
