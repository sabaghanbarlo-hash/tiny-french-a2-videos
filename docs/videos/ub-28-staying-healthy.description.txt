GRAMMAR
The gérondif - Form it with en plus the nous form of the present, with -ons replaced by -ant: nous écoutons becomes en écoutant, nous lisons becomes en lisant, nous faisons becomes en faisant. Three exceptions: être becomes en étant, avoir becomes en ayant, savoir becomes en sachant. The gérondif never changes with the subject.
What the gérondif expresses - It says two actions happen at the same time, or how / by what means something is done. Both verbs must have the same subject. If the subjects differ, use pendant que: je lis pendant que tu cuisines.
