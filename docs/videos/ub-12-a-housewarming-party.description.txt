VOCABULARY
la pendaison de crémaillère (lah pahn-deh-ZOHN duh kray-mah-YEHR) - housewarming party
inviter (an-vee-TAY) - to invite
apporter (ah-pohr-TAY) - to bring
écrire à (ay-KREER ah) - to write to
donner à (doh-NAY ah) - to give to
demander à (duh-mahn-DAY ah) - to ask (someone)
parler à (pahr-LAY ah) - to speak to
quelque chose à boire (kehl-kuh SHOHZ ah BWAHR) - something to drink
ça suffit (sah sew-FEE) - that's enough

GRAMMAR
Indirect object pronouns - Verbs followed by à plus a person use indirect pronouns: me, te, lui, nous, vous, leur. They go before the verb: je t'ai écrit, je lui parle, je leur ai demandé. Lui and leur do not change for gender, and the past participle does not agree with them.
Commands with pronouns - In an affirmative command the pronoun comes after the verb with a hyphen, and me becomes moi: apporte-moi, donne-lui, écris-leur. In a negative command it stays before the verb: ne me donne pas, ne lui parle pas.
